# ADR-003: ML Model Selection – BERT for Classification, LSTM for Forecasting

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-05-19  
**Deciders:** Nana Daasebre, Foureiratou ZAKARI (ML Engineers), Emmanuel Adoum

---

## Context

The platform requires two ML tasks:

1. **Risk classification** – Given a news article, output:
   - MTN relevance (0–100%)
   - One of six risk categories with confidence.
2. **Time‑series forecasting** – Predict 7‑day and 30‑day financial impact (GHS/USD) from historical article risk scores.

Options for classification: BERT, RoBERTa, DistilBERT, traditional SVM/LR.  
Options for forecasting: LSTM, XGBoost, ARIMA, Prophet.

---

## Decision

- **Classification:** Fine‑tune **BERT‑base‑uncased**.
- **Forecasting:** **LSTM** (2‑layer, 64 units) with Monte Carlo simulation for uncertainty.

---

## Rationale

**BERT:**
- State‑of‑the‑art on text classification benchmarks.
- Pre‑trained on general corpus; fine‑tuning on MTN‑specific news (12 months) yields F1 > 0.85 (target).
- Hugging Face Transformers library simplifies deployment.

**LSTM:**
- Captures temporal dependencies in news risk flow (e.g., escalation of regulatory fines).
- Trained on 12 months of daily aggregated risk scores.
- Combined with Monte Carlo provides min/mid/max bands (required by stakeholders).
- XGBoost was considered but does not naturally handle sequential context.

---

## Consequences

- **Positive:** High accuracy, meets project targets.
- **Negative:** BERT inference is computationally heavy (~150 ms per article).  
  Mitigation: batch processing in Celery, GPU instances for production if needed.  
  LSTM retraining monthly requires historical data pipeline.

---

## Alternatives Considered

- **DistilBERT** – Faster but slightly lower accuracy (F1 ~0.82). We prioritise accuracy over speed within the 15‑min window.
- **XGBoost with rolling windows** – Faster but cannot capture long‑range dependencies (e.g., political risk that builds over weeks).
- **Prophet** – Good for seasonal trends but fails on irregular event‑driven shocks.

---

## Compliance

-  F1 > 0.85 target (BERT fine‑tuned)
-  7‑ and 30‑day forecasts (LSTM + Monte Carlo)
-  Financial impact bands (Monte Carlo)
